Biographical Overview

Elena Ferrante is a pseudonymous Italian novelist. Her books, originally published in Italian, have been translated into many languages. Her four-book series of Neapolitan Novels are among her best-known works.

Despite international recognition, she has kept her identity secret since the publication of her first novel in 1992. She has stated that "books, once they are written, have no need of their authors."

Ferrante's work often explores the intense, complex, and sometimes destructive relationships between women, set against the backdrop of a rapidly changing Italian society, particularly in Naples.
